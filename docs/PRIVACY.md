# PocketPad privacy policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what information the PocketPad Android app ("the
app") and the PocketPad desktop receiver handle. Contact:
**[CONTACT EMAIL]**.

## Summary

- Your controller input goes only to your own computer, over your local
  network or Bluetooth. PocketPad has no servers and no accounts.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob on its Settings screen. Google may
  collect information for advertising, as described below.

## Information the app keeps on your phone

| Information | Purpose | Shared? |
|---|---|---|
| Pairing codes and computer addresses you enter or scan | Reconnecting to your computers | No |
| A random device identifier created by the app | Lets your computer recognise this phone | Only with your own computer |
| App settings | Remembering your preferences | No |

This information stays in the app's private storage and is deleted when you
uninstall the app.

## Information sent to your computer

While you use the controller, the app sends button, stick and trigger input,
the phone's battery level and connection timing to the PocketPad receiver on
your computer. Wi-Fi packets are authenticated with your pairing code. In
Bluetooth mode, the phone acts as a standard Bluetooth controller. This data
is never sent to PocketPad or any other party.

## Permissions

| Permission | Use |
|---|---|
| Network and Wi-Fi state | Finding and connecting to your computer; loading ads |
| Camera | Scanning the pairing QR code. Images are processed on the phone and not stored or sent anywhere |
| Bluetooth | Bluetooth controller mode |
| Vibration | Rumble from games and haptic feedback |

## Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show a banner ad on the Settings screen. No ads
are shown on the controller screen. Google may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID;
- device and app information, such as the device model and operating system
  version;
- your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived;
- information about your interaction with ads.

Google uses this information to serve, measure and personalise ads and to
prevent fraud, as described in Google's privacy policy
(<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and in "How Google uses information
from sites or apps that use our services"
(<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app
asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your
choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy options. On any
device, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of
personalised ads, in Android's settings under Privacy > Ads.

## Desktop receiver

The receiver runs on your computer and communicates only with your phone. It
stores its pairing code in your user configuration folder and sends no data
over the internet.

## Children

PocketPad is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.
